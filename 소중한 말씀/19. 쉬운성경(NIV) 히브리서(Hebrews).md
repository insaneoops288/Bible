#### ✟ May the God of peace, who through the blood of the eternal covenant brought back from the dead our Lord Jesus, that great Shepherd of the sheep, -Hebrews(Heb) 13:20-

#### ✟ Grace be with you all. -Hebrews(Heb) 13:25-
